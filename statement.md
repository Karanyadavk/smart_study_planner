# Problem Statement

Students often struggle to manage multiple subjects, assignments, and revision schedules at the same time. Without a structured planning system, they may miss deadlines, spend too much time on weak areas, and fail to track the effort invested in each course.

# Scope

This project focuses on academic planning and productivity tracking for students. It enables them to add subjects, create tasks, log study sessions, and review performance summaries in a single tool.

# Target Users

- College students
- University learners
- Self-paced learners preparing for exams
- Students managing multiple subjects simultaneously

# High-Level Features

- Subject management
- Task scheduling and prioritization
- Study session tracking
- Weekly analytics and progress summaries
- Persistent local storage for academic records
